Learning paths

Courses are grouped into paths in the order a new hire would take them. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Certifications and labs

Certifications are assessed through role-play against a rubric. Labs are built in the learner's own n8n instance and checked by an automatic grader.

How the academy stays current

An n8n workflow watches product releases and keeps the courses in step. Nothing is published without a person reviewing it.

ReleaseA new version of n8n is published.
AnalyseThe workflow maps each change to product areas, roles and courses.
DraftIt drafts the course updates for each role, using their pain points.
ReviewThe enablement lead checks the drafts with product.
PublishApproved courses and this page are updated.

About this concept

How it is organised

A small taxonomy tags every course by role, level, competency, product area and learning moment, so each person gets their own path and certifications map to competencies.

What is real

The structure, the taxonomy and the release workflow are real and running. The course content is illustrative and marked for product review where it makes product claims.
